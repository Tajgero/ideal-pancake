# Analysis

## Layer 6, Head 11

Word "my" is paying attention to MASK, 

Example Sentences:
- Oh no, my very big [MASK] is gone forever!
- My red small [MASK] is missing

## Layer 11, Head 2

A lot of words are paying attention to simple dot at the ending layers. Also "Zombies" are related to "are" and "now", same with "Neighbors" which happens on 2 layers before predecessor.

Example Sentences:
- Zombies are now officialy [MASK].
- Neighbors are the [MASK] creatures.
